I’m a Commerce graduate from the University of Calcutta and an MBA candidate in the Global MBA program associated with RIIM Pune and the University of South Wales. My focus is supply chain management, with interests spanning operations, procurement, logistics, business strategy and marketing.

My exposure to my family’s livestock enterprise gave me an early appreciation for coordination, accountability and everyday business decisions. MBA projects have helped me apply strategic frameworks to company challenges and strengthen my research, analysis and communication.

I have strong knowledge of marketing and negotiation. I enjoy understanding customer and stakeholder needs, communicating value, listening carefully, working through objections and looking for practical, mutually beneficial outcomes. My approach is to prepare, ask questions, understand interests and clarify next steps.

I’m developing thoughtful ways to use AI in marketing and content creation: brainstorming campaign angles, creating first drafts, adapting content for different audiences and formats, and checking accuracy, tone and relevance before use. I treat AI as a starting point and review the final work myself.

Sport has shaped how I work, too. Playing cricket, football, athletics and swimming at institutional and district levels has helped me build discipline, resilience and a team-first mindset. My experience as a swim instructor also strengthened my appreciation for clear instruction, patience and steady progress.

Strategic ManagementMBA Project

Intel Foundry: earning customer trust

Team project asking how Intel could turn advanced manufacturing capabilities into customer confidence, external foundry growth and durable advantage. The analysis used SWOT, VRIO and PESTEL alongside competitor analysis to consider internal strengths and external market pressures.

Key takeaway: technology matters, and so do customer trust, partnerships, execution and sustainable profitability.
AI & Supply ChainDigital Project

AI Supply Chain Control Tower

A project that brings inventory, demand forecasting, supplier performance, purchase orders, logistics, risk monitoring and what-if planning into one workspace. It is designed to help users see operational signals together and explore potential issues before they disrupt flow.

Concept areas: visibility · planning · risk awareness · decision support
E-commerceDigital Experience

Astrotatva

A full-stack consultation and e-commerce platform created for astrology, numerology, Vastu, Puja services and spiritual products. Features include consultation booking, product browsing, accounts, wishlist, cart, order tracking and an admin dashboard.

Built with: HTML · CSS · JavaScript · Vercel APIs · Neon PostgreSQL
